# Privacy Policy

**Stoutheart Holding Doc Flow** — operated by Stoutheart, LLC, a Wyoming limited
liability company. Effective 2026-11-03.

## Scope

This application is a private internal tool. It has no users other than its operator,
collects no information from the public, and serves no third parties. This policy
describes how the operator's own financial data is handled.

## What data is accessed

Transaction records, account balances, account names and masked account numbers, and
account statements, for **bank accounts owned or controlled by Stoutheart, LLC, its
affiliated entities, and its sole member**. Access is obtained through Plaid Inc.,
authorized directly by the account holder.

## What data is not collected

No data is collected from any person other than the operator. There are no customers,
no visitors' data, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising.

## Credentials

Banking credentials are **never seen, handled, transmitted, or stored by this
application.** Authentication takes place entirely within the bank's own or Plaid's
hosted login pages.

## How data is stored

Retrieved financial data is stored locally on hardware controlled by the operator and
is used solely to generate the company's internal corporate and accounting records.

## How data is shared

**Financial data is not sold, licensed, rented, or shared with any third party.** The
only parties with access are the account holder, and the company's accountant and
professional advisors in the ordinary course of preparing the company's books and
tax filings.

## Retention and revocation

Data is retained for as long as the company's recordkeeping and tax obligations
require. The operator may revoke this application's access at any time through Plaid
or directly through the financial institution, which immediately ends further access.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected on this page with a revised
effective date.
